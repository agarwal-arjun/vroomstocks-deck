What the investor actually faces

  • Context gets lost. Moving between screens, filings and spreadsheets breaks the research flow.
  • Assumptions drift. A valuation needs revisiting as financial results change.
  • Funds hide another layer. A fund name alone does not explain its underlying holdings.
  • Monitoring takes effort. Repeating the same checks is not the same as being told what changed.

The consequence

The opportunity is not to claim that other tools do not exist. It is to reduce the effort between finding an idea, understanding it and keeping that understanding current.

What is VroomStocks

Two journeys.
One research workspace.

Built first for self-directed investors researching businesses and understanding their Indian mutual-fund holdings.

Stocks · India + US

Screen → compare → value → track.

Shortlist companies, examine filings and ratios, compare peers in Visual, adjust a DCF, then save a watchlist and follow changes through alerts.

Mutual funds · India

Screen → import → understand → monitor.

Explore direct-growth schemes, import a CAS statement, inspect holdings and underlying company quality, and track value and gain/loss.

Evidence before explanation

Reported financials and defined metrics support the analysis; adjustable assumptions make valuation inspectable. AI summaries help explain the material, but can be wrong or incomplete and should be checked against the source.

Research and analysis only. VroomStocks does not currently place trades or provide investment advice.

What's built so far · product

From a shortlist to an informed view.

1 · Discover

Stock and mutual-fund screeners, saved screens, named watchlists and metric templates. Start with a focused universe, not a wall of numbers.

2 · Understand

Company filings, financial statements and financial ratios, segments/KPIs and year-over-year changes. Visual compares up to six companies; annual, quarterly, biannual and LTM views depend on available data.

3 · Value

Editable DCF assumptions and scenario comparisons. Q-Score adds a defined quality-versus-valuation lens; AI insight explains valuation, fundamentals, technicals and options.

4 · Monitor

Earnings calendar, transcripts and call summaries; exchange announcements, insider/shareholding data and email digests. Follow price moves, technical signals and stock or fund screener changes.

5 · Test

Technical charts, strategy backtests, paper trading and watchlist-wide strategy analytics. Options analytics cover US names; India availability varies. Simulated performance is not a forecast.

Across your funds

Indian direct-growth fund screening, CAS import, NAV-based portfolio tracking and holdings look-through. Broader cross-asset portfolio insights remain on the roadmap.

Q-Score = Q-Green-Score − Q-Black-Score, ranging from −100 to +100: quality less valuation expensiveness, not a predicted return. Product guide · Coverage notes. Public documentation reviewed 11 Oct 2026; feature and history availability vary by company and plan.

Appendix · data platform and diligence

Data quality is an ongoing discipline.

Architecture described by the team

  • Flask + Azure SQL on Azure App Service, custom domain, PWA-installable.
  • Private workspaces — account-scoped watchlists and settings; isolation requires ongoing verification.
  • Ingestion and caching — company filings, market feeds and fund disclosures, with fallback sources and cached data to reduce upstream dependence.
  • Operations — background sync, retry tracking and telemetry. Source outages and coverage gaps remain possible.

Evidence to bring to diligence

  • Coverage and freshness by market, company, period and metric; distinguish missing data from zero.
  • Traceability from displayed financials to filings; definitions and assumptions for derived metrics.
  • Measured reliability — dated availability, API latency and sync success reports, not a blanket guarantee.
  • Commercial readiness — provider rights, account isolation checks, AI evaluation and service commitments before enterprise launch.

Architecture statements are team descriptions, not an independent code or infrastructure audit. No uptime, complete-coverage or absolute-isolation guarantee is asserted. A prior live capture of fundamental charts returned a market-mismatch error; that path needs rechecking before a demo.
